A swank environment

More than a restaurant. It's a vibe.

The Flavor House is where Trinidad kitchens meet a downtown Dayton corner. Scotch bonnet, green seasoning, and slow-stewed oxtail, woven through with Creole and soul-food tradition, and served in a room designed for a slow evening and a good crowd.

Come for a lunch plate. Stay for a craft cocktail and real hospitality. Warm pendant light, low music, and a unique dining experience with flavorful, from-scratch food and superior service.

The Kitchen

Caribbean & Creole

Cooked from scratch. Gumbo, oxtail, rum-glazed salmon, curry roti, and Signature baked mac. No shortcuts.

The Lounge

Cocktail Culture

A full bar of house cocktails and top-shelf spirits. Happy Hour every day, 4 to 6PM, half off the good stuff.

The Patio & Room

Cigars & Celebrations

A cigar patio and a connected private room built for showers, mixers, and the whole family.

The plates we're known for.

A handful of the dishes regulars come back for, and the ones that keep the foodie tastings talking. Every plate is built from scratch, spiced with intent, and portioned for second helpings.

    Crab legs, chicken, andouille, shrimp, and okra with onions and peppers, ladled over yellow Caribbean rice.

  • Oxtail Sandwich ★

    Slow-braised oxtail and dijon on toasted brioche, served with fries. The Trinidad classic, handheld.

  • Caribbean Rum Glazed Salmon ★

    Grilled rum-glazed salmon over garlic mash with green beans. Sweet heat, clean finish.

  • Portobello Dian ★

    Grilled portobello with onions and peppers over Caribbean rice, finished in parmesan cream. The vegetarian showpiece.
