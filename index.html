A swank environment

More than a restaurant. It's a vibe.

Around here, comfort is seasoned with culture. Our Creole comfort cuisine is rooted in tradition, inspired by culture, and built with bold flavor. Every dish is prepared with care, every cocktail is handcrafted, and every visit is an invitation to gather, connect, and stay a while.

From rich, soulful entrées to signature cocktails and live music in The Flavor Room, we serve more than a meal. Whether you are discovering us for the first time or coming back again, there is always a seat waiting for you. Come for the flavor. Stay for the experience.

From the kitchen

The plates we're known for.

A handful of the dishes regulars come back for, and the ones that keep the foodie tastings talking. Every plate is built from scratch, spiced with intent, and portioned for second helpings. Chef specials rotate regularly, ask your server what's featured today.
